Glossary & FAQs

Plain-language compliance guidance

Clear explanations for terms that are often misunderstood in audits, corrective action and management systems.

What is the difference between a correction and corrective action?

A correction addresses the immediate problem. Corrective action addresses the cause of the nonconformity so that recurrence is prevented or reduced.

What is objective evidence in an audit?

Information that can be verified, such as records, observations, system data, interviews supported by evidence and documented results. An unsupported statement is not strong evidence on its own.

What does CAPA mean?

CAPA refers to corrective and preventive action. In modern ISO management-system practice, the focus is on correcting nonconformities, addressing causes and using risk-based thinking to prevent unwanted outcomes.

What is an RTO?

Recovery Time Objective is the target time for restoring a disrupted activity, service or resource to an acceptable level after disruption.

What is an environmental aspect?

An environmental aspect is an element of an organisation's activities, products or services that interacts or can interact with the environment.

When should a management system document be reviewed?

Review frequency should reflect organisational requirements, risk, changes and applicable obligations. A fixed cycle may be useful, but material changes can require review before the scheduled date.
