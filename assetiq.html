Platform Features

Every asset. Every record.

From the moment an asset is registered to its end of life, every inspection, maintenance event and compliance record is tracked and accessible.

Asset Register
A complete, searchable register of every asset — equipment, vehicles, facilities and infrastructure — with classification, location, owner and status.
Inspection Tracking
Schedule and record inspections per asset. Track findings, assign corrective actions and maintain a full inspection history for audit evidence.
Maintenance Schedules
Preventive and corrective maintenance schedules with automated reminders. Know what is due, who is responsible and whether it was completed on time.
Lifecycle Records
Full asset lifecycle visibility from acquisition through service to disposal. Supporting better capital planning and replacement decisions.
Compliance Visibility
Identify assets with overdue inspections or maintenance. Dashboard flags compliance gaps before they become audit findings or operational failures.
Reports and Exports
Asset status reports, inspection summaries and maintenance histories — exportable to PDF and Excel for management reviews and auditor evidence.
