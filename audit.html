Platform Features

From schedule to closure.

Plan your audit programme, execute audits, raise findings and track every corrective action to closure — all in one structured, evidence-based system.

Audit Scheduling
Build an annual audit programme with scheduled audit dates, scope, auditors and processes. Automated reminders keep the schedule on track throughout the year.
Auditor Assignment
Assign lead auditors and audit team members per audit. Maintain auditor profiles with qualifications, availability and audit history.
Findings Management
Raise nonconformities, observations and opportunities for improvement directly within the audit. Each finding is categorised, graded and linked to its source audit.
CAPA Linkage
Findings automatically generate corrective actions in the CAPA register. Track root cause analysis, corrective action, evidence and closure without switching systems.
Audit Reports
Generate structured audit reports with findings summary, audit scope, attendance and corrective action status — ready for management review and external auditor review.
Evidence Management
Attach audit evidence — photos, documents, completed checklists — directly to audit records. Full evidence trail from audit planning through to corrective action closure.
