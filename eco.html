Platform Features

Manage what matters most.

Track every environmental obligation, objective and performance indicator in one structured system — so your organisation can demonstrate accountability with confidence.

Environmental Aspects Register
Identify, categorise and evaluate environmental aspects and their significance. Maintain a structured register that satisfies ISO 14001 clause 6.1.2 requirements.
Compliance Obligations
Track all legal, regulatory and voluntary environmental obligations. Set review dates and document how compliance is being maintained and demonstrated.
Environmental Objectives
Set, assign and monitor environmental objectives and targets. Track progress and record evidence of actions taken to meet sustainability goals.
ISO 14001 Support
Built around the requirements of ISO 14001. Each module maps to relevant clauses — making audit preparation and management review inputs straightforward.
Performance Monitoring
Track environmental performance indicators over time. Dashboard charts and trend data give managers real visibility into environmental improvement progress.
Reports and Evidence
Generate compliance summaries, aspect registers and objective progress reports — ready for management reviews, external audits and regulatory inspections.
